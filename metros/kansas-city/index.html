Kansas City

H.O.O.D. Hope's founding metro and organizational headquarters — where the Sustainable Wellness Housing Program was born and where the model is being proven alongside St. Louis.

Kansas City is home base — the metro where H.O.O.D. Hope was founded, headquartered at 710 Central Street, and where the 5-Stage Path to Homeownership is being built from the ground up.
