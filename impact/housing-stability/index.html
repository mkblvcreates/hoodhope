Impact category

Housing Stability

We help residents avoid housing crises before they start, and rebuild stability after one. That includes eviction prevention navigation, transitional housing partner connections, homeownership readiness, financial education, and long-term independence planning — coordinated with trusted local partners in every metro.

Find Help Partner With Us
Who this serves Families, Individuals facing housing instability, Older adults, Veterans

Example initiatives

What this looks like in practice.

  • Eviction prevention referrals
  • Transitional housing partners
  • Home stability navigation
  • Homeownership and financial education readiness
